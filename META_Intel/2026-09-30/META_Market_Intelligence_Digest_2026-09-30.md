- **Published:** TrendForce Press Center, 30 Sep 2026 (Taipei; date only). Every 30 Sep Taipei time up to 15:57 falls in the window, and the page was live when checked.
- **Summary (TrendForce estimates):**
  - 4Q26 contract prices: conventional DRAM +10–15% QoQ (a slower pace), NAND +15–20% QoQ.
