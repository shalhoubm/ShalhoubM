*Applying Semiconductor and Server Tariffs to Data Centers, Under Section 232 or Section 301, Would Now Cost the U.S. About $180 Billion a Year*

* A 25% tariff on semiconductors and servers would equal a 15.6% effective tax on data center construction, putting roughly $900 billion of planned 2027 to 2031 U.S. data center investment at risk
* The estimated annual cost is about $180 billion, or about 0.56% of U.S. GDP, with roughly 474,000 American jobs jeopardized
* Dell'Oro Group now projects worldwide data center capital spending above $3 trillion by 2030, nearly double its January forecast
