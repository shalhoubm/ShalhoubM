## Executive headline

Monday was a two-speed day. Globally, Dario Amodei's weekend call to slow frontier AI triggered the sharpest AI-hardware sell-off since July: the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 5.8%, SK hynix 7.3% and the KOSPI 3.26%. Yet the component data published the same day points the other way: Omdia put 2Q26 semiconductor revenue at a record $425bn with memory above half of it, TrendForce guided high-capacity NOR up 90–110% in 2H26, and Korea's August chip exports rose 209%. Sentiment wobbled; bill-of-materials costs did not. In the Kingdom, sovereign compute moved from build to monetisation: HUMAIN Cloud will front KAUST's Shaheen III HPC as a service, and Savills says live Saudi IT power is up 57% since 2024. Meanwhile competitors worked the channel: Dell signed Salam for cloud and edge AI, Oracle brought Mannai into KSA, and Digital Realty entered Türkiye. **Lenovo META read:** IDC's #1 x86 server shipment ranking lifted Lenovo shares 4.6% today. That credential has to be converted in KSA telco and sovereign-cloud accounts now, before Dell's partner lock-ins set. Every other META compute announcement today left the server supplier undisclosed.
