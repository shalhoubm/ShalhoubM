A thin news day in KSA ahead of National Day (23 Sep). The signal that matters came from the PC market, not the Gulf.
- **PC demand:** HP filed an 8-K saying it expects industry PC units to fall mid-single digits in 2027. It withheld FY27 guidance.

**Lenovo META read:** Two moves.
- **ISG:** HUMAIN's compute is being contracted now, with the MIS 250MW EPC signed 19 Sep and no hardware vendor named. ISG should be in front of MIS and HUMAIN this week.
- **IDG:** HP's retreat on 2027 volumes is the opening to take META commercial PC share on availability, but only if 2027 memory supply is locked first.

**1. HUMAIN's build-out has moved into contracting, and the compute vendor slot is still open.**
- **What changed:** MIS signed the definitive EPC for HUMAIN's AI data centres on 19 Sep, expanding scope fivefold from 50MW to 250MW for over SAR 8.76bn. Execution will run through sequential work orders, each disclosed on Tadawul. The next day NVIDIA told Al-Riyadh that the Kingdom is "in operation", restating 500MW and an 18,000-GB300 supercomputer. Neither names a server OEM.
- **Why it matters:** EPC work orders come before IT fit-out. The window to be specified into rack, liquid-cooling and integration designs is the next one or two quarters, not after the halls are built.
- **Action:** ISG KSA lead, with the RHQ to confirm the individual, to secure a technical session with MIS's EPC team and HUMAIN's infrastructure office within two weeks. Position Neptune direct-water cooling plus Riyadh-assembled racks as the localisation answer. Set a Tadawul alert on MIS (7200) for each work order.

**2. The PC market leader's closest rival just told investors 2027 will shrink, and memory is the reason.**
- **What changed:** HP's 8-K assumes 2027 PC units fall mid-single digits and withholds FY27 guidance. IDC (via the Guardian) sees memory costs up more than 300% and a record ~17% fall in smartphone sales. TrendForce says Q4 eSSD orders may exceed Q3. Chinese phone makers (Xiaomi, Vivo) are passing costs through openly.
- **Why it matters:** in a shrinking, cost-driven market, share goes to whoever can deliver. HP is signalling margin defence. Lenovo's META IDG can win 2027 commercial refreshes (KSA government and education, UAE banks) if memory allocation is secured and quotes are protected against repricing.
- **Action:**
  - META IDG GM and supply planning: confirm 2027 DRAM and NAND allocation for META commercial SKUs this month.
  - Shorten quote validity on ISG storage and servers to 14 days until Micron's 30 Sep print.
  - Motorola: keep pricing matched to Xiaomi and Vivo increases in the GCC and Kenya rather than absorbing costs.

**3. The Gulf's sovereign buyers are consolidating and choosing technology stacks.**
- **What changed:** IHC formally placed Tahnoon's empire under Fount Trust. Rest of World reports G42 leaning into NVIDIA's free-model push. DeepSeek says Huawei training chips arrive in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027. And the Trump–Xi dinner on 24 Sep will put the US AI CEOs in the room.
- **Why it matters:** the UAE is locking into a US stack with more centralised purchasing, while Huawei's training-grade Ascend strengthens its pitch to non-aligned META buyers in Africa and Central Asia. Lenovo sits between the two camps and needs a crisp compliance and origin narrative for each.
- **Action:** RHQ Government Affairs with ISG marketing to refresh the "trusted, compliant, localised" brief for UAE sovereign accounts before Q4 tenders. Read the Trump–Xi outcome on 24–25 Sep for chip-export clauses.
